# Privacy Policy for Wizard Wars

**Last updated: June 16, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed by "Wizard Wars" ("we", "us", or "our"). By accessing or using our game, you signify that you have read, understood, and agree to our collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect
We strive to collect as little data as possible. "Wizard Wars" is designed to be played offline; however, we collect the following types of information:

* **Automatically Collected Information:** When you use the game, we may automatically collect certain information, including your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers. This is primarily for the purpose of serving advertisements.
* **In-App Purchase Information:** When you make an in-game purchase, we do not store your credit card or payment information. All payment transactions are processed through the Google Play Store, and we only receive confirmation of the purchase from Google.

## 2. How We Use Information
We use the information we collect for the following purposes:
* To facilitate in-game purchases.
* To serve advertisements through Google AdSense/AdMob to support the development of the game.
* To improve the game's performance and analyze usage trends.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:
* **Google AdSense/AdMob:** We use Google's advertising services to show ads within the game. Please review Google's [Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy) to understand how they collect and use data.
* **Google Play Store:** Your transactions are handled by the Google Play Store. Please review Google's [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms) for more information.

## 4. Data Security
We implement reasonable security measures to protect the security of your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Our game is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
